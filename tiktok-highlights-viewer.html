TikTok Highlights Viewer

View the story highlights a public account has saved.

Stories vanish after 24 hours — unless the account pins them as highlights, which live on permanently under the profile. Highlights are where businesses keep their offers and creators keep their greatest hits. The TikTok Highlights Viewer plays those saved highlights in your browser without a login, so you can browse an account's curated archive from any device. For anyone doing due diligence on a brand, this is the fastest way to read a company's story archive in one sitting.

How to use it

  1. Enter the username of the public TikTok account.
  2. Open the highlights section on the account's profile page.
  3. Pick a highlight collection and watch it start to finish.

Good to know

Only highlights the account chose to keep are visible — deleted highlights are gone, and expired stories that were never saved can't be brought back by any tool. Private accounts are excluded entirely. Since highlights are permanent public content by the account's own choice, this is the least time-sensitive tool in the set.
