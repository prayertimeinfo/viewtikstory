TikTok Story Downloader

Save stories to your device before the 24-hour expiry.

Watching is one thing; keeping is another. The TikTok Story Downloader saves a public story to your phone or computer as a video file, so it is still there after the original disappears. People use it to archive a funny clip, keep a reference, or repost something they have permission to share — before the 24-hour window closes forever. Creators also use it defensively: saving your own story is the only reliable backup, since TikTok deletes the original on expiry.

How to use it

  1. Open the story you want to keep and copy its link, or enter the account's username.
  2. Let the tool load the active story and its download button.
  3. Download the file and check it plays correctly on your device.

Good to know

Downloads only work for stories that are still live — nothing can resurrect an expired story. Only public stories are downloadable; private accounts are excluded by design. And a saved copy is for personal use: reposting someone else's story as your own is their work, not yours, even if the file is on your phone.
