TikTok PFP Downloader

View and download profile pictures at full size.

In the app, a profile picture is a tiny cropped circle you can't zoom into. The TikTok PFP Downloader pulls the original full-size version of a public account's picture and shows it at full resolution — with a download button if you want to keep it. Designers use it for reference, curious viewers use it to actually see the photo behind the thumbnail. It also settles the small mystery of whether a brand changed its logo overnight: the full-size image shows details a 98-pixel circle never will.

How to use it

  1. Enter the username of the public TikTok account.
  2. Wait for the full-size profile picture to render on screen.
  3. Download the image file if you want to keep it.

Good to know

Only public accounts work; private profiles reveal nothing. The quality you get is whatever the account uploaded — if they uploaded a low-resolution picture, no tool can sharpen it into a new one. And as with any image of a person, saving it is fine, but reusing someone's face without permission is not.
