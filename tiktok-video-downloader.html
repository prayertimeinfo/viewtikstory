TikTok Video Downloader

Download public TikTok videos without the watermark.

TikTok's own "Save video" option stamps a bouncing watermark across the clip. The TikTok Video Downloader fetches the clean version of a public video instead — the same file, minus the watermark, saved straight to your device. It is the most-used tool of its kind, because offline copies, compilations, and re-edits all start with a clean file. Students of video editing also use it to study transitions and pacing frame by frame, without the watermark bouncing over the exact pixels they are analyzing.

How to use it

  1. Copy the link of the TikTok video you want to save.
  2. Paste it into the downloader and let it process the video.
  3. Choose the no-watermark version and download the file.

Good to know

Only public videos can be downloaded — if the account is private or the video was deleted, there is nothing to fetch. Videos whose creators disabled downloads won't be available either. A clean file is a convenience, not a license: credit the creator and don't re-upload other people's videos as if they were yours.
