TikTok Comment Viewer

Read the comments on a video without opening the app.

The comment section is often better than the video — context, jokes, corrections, the first person who actually answered the question. The TikTok Comment Viewer loads the comments of a public TikTok video as a plain, readable list in your browser. No login, no app, no endless vertical scroll if all you want is to read what people said. Journalists use it to capture public reaction to a post, and moderators use it to review a thread quickly from a work computer without installing anything.

How to use it

  1. Copy the link of the TikTok video.
  2. Paste it into the viewer and let the comments load.
  3. Scroll through the list — and search or filter if the tool offers it.

Good to know

Only public videos are readable; comments on private accounts stay private. Videos with comments disabled show nothing, and the list is read-only — you can't reply or like without a TikTok account. Very long threads may load in pages rather than all at once, which is the app doing it too, just less visibly.
