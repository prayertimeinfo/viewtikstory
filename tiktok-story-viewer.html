TikTok Story Viewer

Watch public stories anonymously, without an account.

The TikTok Story Viewer is the flagship of the family: paste a username, and it plays that account's active stories in your browser. No TikTok account, no app install, and no entry in the story's viewer list. It is the tool most people mean when they say "anonymous TikTok story viewer." It also covers the natural edge cases: stories that disappeared mid-session, accounts that posted several clips in a row, and the difference between viewing from a desktop versus a phone screen.

How to use it

  1. Enter the TikTok username of a public account.
  2. Wait a moment while the tool fetches the account's active stories.
  3. Watch them back-to-back in your browser — tap through like you would in the app.

Good to know

Stories only exist for 24 hours; the viewer can only show what is currently active. Private accounts never appear, and an account with no active stories simply shows nothing. Your views are not recorded because there is no account to record them against — but this also means you cannot like, reply, or interact with the story from here.
