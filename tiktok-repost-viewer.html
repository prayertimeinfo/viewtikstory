TikTok Repost Viewer

See which videos a public account has reposted.

Reposts are a window into someone's taste: the videos they amplified without posting themselves. TikTok buries that tab a little, and the app only shows it when you're logged in and looking at the right place. The TikTok Repost Viewer lists a public account's reposts in your browser — a quick way to map what an account amplifies, from brands tracking mentions to researchers sampling trends. It also reveals the quiet half of an influencer's output: what they promote but didn't create.

How to use it

  1. Enter the username of the public TikTok account.
  2. Let the tool load the account's reposted videos.
  3. Browse the list and open any repost that stands out.

Good to know

Only public accounts work, and only reposts the account left public appear. If an account never reposts, or removed its reposts, the list will be empty — that is information too. TikTok doesn't notify anyone that their reposts were viewed this way, since no account is attached to the visit.
