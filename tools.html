All TikTok Tools

Every tool in the family, explained in one page.

These are free web tools that work with TikTok's public content only: no login, no app, nothing installed. Each one does exactly one job. Every page below explains what the tool does, how to use it in three steps, and — most importantly — what it honestly can't do.

  • TikTok Story Viewer
    Watch public TikTok stories anonymously, without an account.
  • TikTok Story Downloader
    Save stories to your device before the 24-hour expiry.
  • TikTok Video Downloader
    Download public TikTok videos without the watermark.
  • TikTok Profile Viewer
    Browse public TikTok profiles anonymously, right in your browser.
  • TikTok PFP Downloader
    View and download profile pictures at full size.
  • TikTok Photo Downloader
    Save photos from TikTok slideshow posts.
  • TikTok MP3 Downloader
    Extract the audio from a TikTok video as an MP3 file.
  • TikTok Comment Viewer
    Read the comments on a video without opening the app.
  • TikTok Repost Viewer
    See which videos a public account has reposted.
  • TikTok Highlights Viewer
    View the story highlights a public account has saved.
  • TikTok Follower Counter
    Check live follower counts for any public account.
  • TikTok Username Finder
    Find a TikTok username or check whether a handle is taken.

One rule covers all of them: private accounts and deleted content are off-limits everywhere. If a tool's page doesn't say what you want to hear, that is the tool being honest.
