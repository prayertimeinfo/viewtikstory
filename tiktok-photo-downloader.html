TikTok Photo Downloader

Save photos from TikTok slideshow posts.

Not everything on TikTok is a video — photo mode posts string images together with music. The TikTok Photo Downloader extracts each image from a public slideshow post as a separate picture file you can save. It's the tool for anyone who found a carousel of wallpapers, outfits, or infographics and wants the originals. Photographers and mood-board builders use it to collect reference images at their native resolution instead of screenshotting each slide individually.

How to use it

  1. Copy the link of the TikTok slideshow post.
  2. Paste it into the downloader and let it extract the images.
  3. Save each photo individually to your device.

Good to know

This works only with public posts; private and deleted posts can't be fetched. Single-photo cover images that are part of a video post aren't included — only actual slideshow posts. Photos arrive at the resolution TikTok stores them in, and the usual credit rules apply: someone made those images.
